Verbal Ability – Reading Comprehension (1–35)
Passage 1 (Q1–3)

Text:
The rapid expansion of urban areas has created unprecedented environmental challenges. Cities struggle with air pollution, waste management, and water scarcity. Technological solutions, such as smart grids and renewable energy, offer potential mitigation, yet financial and political barriers often impede implementation. Urban planners must balance sustainability with socio-economic realities to ensure long-term resilience.

1. Main idea:
A. Urban technology solves all city problems
B. Cities face environmental challenges requiring integrated planning
C. Political barriers prevent all urban development
D. Urban areas should focus solely on economic growth

2. Urban planning is important because:
A. It increases population density
B. It integrates sustainability with socio-economic realities
C. It builds more skyscrapers
D. It reduces financial barriers automatically

3. What can be inferred about technological solutions?
A. Easy to implement in all cities
B. Unnecessary in urban areas
C. Promising but face financial and political challenges
D. Solve all urban environmental problems

5. Which strategy is NOT mentioned for conservation?

6. Author’s purpose:
A. Criticize urban planning
B. Inform about biodiversity and conservation
C. Argue against human settlements
D. Promote industrialization

Passage 12 (Q31–32)

Text:
Ang pagbabago ng klima ay nagdudulot ng matinding epekto sa kalikasan at lipunan. Ang pagbaha, tagtuyot, at pag-init ng mundo ay nakaaapekto sa agrikultura, kalusugan, at kabuhayan. Kinakailangan ng agarang aksyon mula sa pamahalaan at mamamayan upang mapagaan ang epekto nito.

31. Pangunahing ideya?

D. Pagtatayo ng mga gusali lamang
