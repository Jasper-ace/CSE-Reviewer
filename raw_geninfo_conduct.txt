# General Information

## Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees (R.A. 6713)

1. RA 6713 is also known as the Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees because it primarily aims to:
   A. Regulate salaries of public officials
   B. Establish rules for political parties
   C. Promote high ethical standards in public service
   D. Manage government procurement

2. Under RA 6713, “Government” includes:
   A. Only national government agencies
   B. National, local, GOCCs and subsidiaries
   C. Only elected officials
   D. Only civil servants

3. A “public official” under the law refers to:
   A. Only regular employees
   B. Only elected officials
   C. Elective and appointive officials and employees
   D. Only military personnel

4. A “gift” under RA 6713 is defined as:
   A. Any item received from relatives
   B. Anything received gratuitously or liberally
   C. Salary increases
   D. Government-issued benefits

5. “Conflict of interest” arises when a public official:
   A. Uses government documents
   B. Has a private interest that may affect official duty
   C. Works overtime
   D. Travels abroad

6. The policy of the State under RA 6713 is to make public officials:
   A. Wealthier than private citizens
   B. Accountable to the people with integrity and loyalty
   C. Free from any scrutiny
   D. Exclusive to political party lines

7. A relative under RA 6713 includes:
   A. Siblings only
   B. All persons related within the fourth civil degree
   C. Employers
   D. Neighbors

8. Which of the following is included in the Act’s norms of conduct?
   A. Personal wealth accumulation
   B. Loyalty to party over country
   C. Political neutrality
   D. Bias in service delivery

9. Simple living in the law means that officials:
   A. May display wealth if they want
   B. Shall not indulge in extravagant or ostentatious display of wealth
   C. Should live beyond their income

10. Nationalism and patriotism in RA 6713 require officials to:
    A. Prioritize foreign companies
    B. Promote use of locally produced goods
    C. Only work for their hometown
    D. Disregard national interest

11. Commitment to public interest means officials must:
    A. Use their office for personal gain
    B. Uphold public interest over personal interest
    C. Refuse service to others
    D. Delegate duties to relatives

12. Professionalism in RA 6713 expects officials to:
    A. Work only when supervised
    B. Perform duties with devotion, excellence, and skill
    C. Avoid public service
    D. Seek undue patronage

13. Justness and sincerity require officials to:
    A. Discriminate against others
    B. Respect rights of all and avoid acts contrary to public interest

14. Political neutrality in service means:
    A. Favoring people of same party
    B. Delivering services to all fairly

15. Responsiveness includes:
    A. Delaying responses to requests

16. Nationalism under RA 6713 also means:
    A. Promote foreign interests
    B. Defend Philippine sovereignty

17. Commitment to democracy includes:
    A. Loyalty to party over country
    B. Upholding public accountability and Constitution

18. Simple living is a standard that prohibits:
    A. Modest lifestyle
    B. Ostentatious display of wealth
    C. Frugality
    D. Ethical conduct

19. RA 6713 requires public officials to be:
    A. Loyal only to family
    B. Accountable to the people
    C. Indifferent to citizens
    D. Focused on external affiliations

20. Public officials must:
    A. Serve only constituents

### Answer Key – RA 6713

1. C – Promote high ethical standards in public service.
2. B – Covers national, local, GOCCs, and subsidiaries.
3. C – Includes elective and appointive officials/employees.
4. B – Anything received gratuitously or by liberality.
5. B – Private interest affecting official duty = conflict.
6. B – Officials must be accountable, loyal, patriotic.
7. B – Relatives within fourth civil degree.
8. C – Political neutrality is required.
9. B – Officials must avoid extravagant wealth display.
10. B – Promote use of locally produced goods.
11. B – Public interest comes before personal interest.
12. B – Duties performed with devotion and skill.
13. B – Respect all and avoid acts against public interest.
14. B – Serve all fairly, politically neutral.
15. B – Provide prompt, courteous service.
16. B – Defend Philippine sovereignty.
17. B – Uphold Constitution and public accountability.
18. B – No ostentatious display of wealth.
19. B – Officials must be accountable to the people.
20. B – Serve all impartially.
21. C – Respond to letters within 15 working days.
22. B – Submit annual reports within 45 working days.
23. B – Official papers processed expeditiously.
24. B – Documents must be accessible to the public.
25. B – Public transactions handled promptly.
26. B – Disclose relatives for transparency.
27. B – SALN avoids conflicts of interest.
28. B – Statements available after 10 working days.
29. B – Reasonable reproduction fees allowed.
30. B – Requests processed promptly.
31. B – Accepting gifts expecting favors is prohibited.
32. B – Cannot have financial interest in matters needing approval.
33. A – Outside employment conflicting with duties is prohibited.
34. B – Misuse of confidential information is prohibited.
35. B – Cannot recommend enterprises you regulate.
36. B – Prohibited acts continue 1 year after separation.
37. B – Violators may face imprisonment and/or fine.
38. B – Private conspirators liable too.
39. B – Administrative penalties include removal/dismissal.
40. B – Civil Service Commission enforces RA 6713.
41. B – Simple living prohibits ostentation.
42. B – Political neutrality forbids preference by party.
43. B – Uphold Constitution and accountability.
44. A – Delays violate responsiveness.
45. B – Services delivered promptly and courteously.
46. B – Avoid wasting public funds; prioritize public interest.
47. B – Misusing confidential info is prohibited.
48. B – Conflicting outside employment is prohibited.
49. B – Incentives motivate ethical conduct.
50. B – Divest conflicting interests voluntarily.
