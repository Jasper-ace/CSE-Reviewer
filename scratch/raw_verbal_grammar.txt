Verbal Ability
(Grammar and Correct Usage) – Answer Key & Explanations
PART I – ENGLISH GRAMMAR & USAGE (1–35)

1. B – “Neither of the students has submitted his or her paper.”
Explanation: Neither is singular; therefore, use has and the singular pronoun his or her.

2. B – Error: are
Explanation: “Each” is singular, so it should be is required, not are required.

3. B – “I wish I were taller.”
Explanation: Subjunctive mood uses were for hypothetical or unreal situations.

4. B – “He didn’t see anybody.”
Explanation: Standard English uses anybody in negative sentences; double negatives are incorrect.

5. B – “She has gone to the market.”
Explanation: Present perfect requires gone, not went.

6. B – “Every student must submit his or her essay.”
Explanation: Singular subject every student takes a singular pronoun.

7. B – “He is good at playing chess.”
Explanation: Correct expression is good at.

8. B – “The number of students is increasing.”
Explanation: The subject is The number, which is singular.

9. B – Error: to go
Explanation: Correct form: “She suggested going to the museum tomorrow.”

10. B – “I look forward to meeting you.”
Explanation: “Look forward to” is followed by a gerund (-ing form).

11. A – “Fewer people attended the lecture than last week.”
Explanation: Fewer is used for countable nouns.

12. B – “This problem is easier than the previous one.”
Explanation: Comparative of easy is easier.

13. B – Error: has
Explanation: The relative clause refers to plural men, so it should be have.

14. D – All of the above
Explanation: The adverb can be correctly placed in all three positions.

15. A – “I studied hard, so I passed the exam.”
Explanation: So correctly expresses cause and effect.

16. A – “Walking down the street, the flowers were beautiful.”
Explanation: Dangling modifier.
Correction: “Walking down the street, I saw beautiful flowers.”

17. B – Criterion → Criteria
Explanation: Singular criterion, plural criteria.

18. B – “He is taller than I.”
Explanation: Grammatically correct; implied phrase is “than I am.”

19. B – “It’s raining outside.”
Explanation: It’s = it is; its is possessive.

20. D – No error
Explanation: Commas correctly separate the appositive phrase.

21. B – “If I had known, I would have helped.”
Explanation: Correct third conditional structure.

22. B – “The book which I borrowed is on the table.”
Explanation: Which correctly refers to a thing.

23. C – “By the time he arrives, we will have already finished dinner.”
Explanation: Future perfect tense is required.

24. B – Error: interesting
Explanation: Should be interested.

25. A – “She let the cat out of the bag.”
Explanation: Idiom meaning to reveal a secret.

26. B – “I saw an eagle flying.”
Explanation: Use an before a vowel sound.

27. B – “He doesn’t like spicy food.”
Explanation: Third-person singular uses doesn’t.

28. A – Dangling modifier: “Running quickly, the finish line was reached.”
Explanation: The subject should be the person running, not the finish line.

29. C – “She likes reading, swimming, and cooking.”
Explanation: Correct parallel structure using all -ing forms.

30. B – “I couldn’t care less about his opinion.”
Explanation: Standard idiom meaning no concern at all.

31. B – “He is responsible for the project.”
Explanation: Correct preposition is for.

32. A – “She insisted that he attend the meeting.”
Explanation: Subjunctive mood uses the base form of the verb.

33. D – Both A and C
Explanation: Both expressions are grammatically correct.

34. C – Error: goes
Explanation: After “suggested that,” use the base form go.

35. A – “Had I known, I would have attended.”
Explanation: Correct inversion of the third conditional.

PART II – FILIPINO GRAMMAR & USAGE (36–50)

36. A – “ay”
Explanation: Correct singular linking verb.

37. A – “saka”
Explanation: Correct connector for sequential actions.

38. A – “siya”
Explanation: Correct subject pronoun for a singular third person.

39. B – “maging”
Explanation: Correct form for a hypothetical condition.

40. C – “sa ilalim”
Explanation: Correct prepositional phrase indicating location.

41. A – “ay”
Explanation: Correct linking verb for a compound subject.

42. D – “sa kanya”
Explanation: Correct comparative phrase: “Masipag si Ana kaysa sa kanya.”

43. B – “sumulat”
Explanation: Correct verb form for completed action.

44. B – “nabagay”
Explanation: Correct participle to match “kulay na nabagay sa silid.”

45. B – “kundi”
Explanation: Correct structure is “Hindi lamang... kundi...”

46. A – “Sino”
Explanation: Interrogative word used for a person or subject.

47. C – “kaysa sa”
Explanation: Correct comparative expression.

48. A – “na”
Explanation: Correct linker between noun and clause.

49. A – “upang”
Explanation: Correct purpose connector.

50. A – “bago”
Explanation: Action occurred before the rain.
