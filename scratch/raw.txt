Numerical Ability – Answers with Solutions
1. B (2)
Given:
2x + 3y = 13
4x − y = 5
From second equation:
y = 4x − 5
Substitute:
2x + 3(4x − 5) = 13
2x + 12x − 15 = 13
14x = 28
x = 2

2. A (2 & 5)
x² − 7x + 10 = 0
Factor:
(x − 5)(x − 2) = 0
x = 5 or x = 2

3. A (34)
Given:
x + 1/x = 6
Square both sides:
36 = x² + 2 + 1/x²
x² + 1/x² = 36 − 2
= 34

4. A (1)

3/x = 3
x = 1
