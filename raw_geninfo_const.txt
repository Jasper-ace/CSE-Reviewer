General Information – Philippine Constitution (Answer Key & Explanations)
1. E

Explanation: According to the Philippine Constitution (Article IX, Section 7 of the 1987 Constitution), public officials and employees cannot accept gifts, titles, or compensation from any foreign government without the consent of Congress.

2. D

Explanation: Cultural rights pertain to the right of individuals and communities to enjoy their culture, use their language, and express themselves artistically and intellectually, while contributing to national heritage and identity.

3. C

Explanation: This right ensures that laws apply equally to all people who are in similar circumstances, preventing discriminatory treatment and upholding fairness under the law.

4. E

Explanation: The right to privacy protects individuals from unnecessary or unauthorized intrusion into their personal life, including being subjected to public exposure without consent.

5. E

Explanation: To vote in Philippine elections, one must be a resident of the Philippines for at least one year and a resident of the place where they intend to vote for at least six months before the election. So, if a person has been a resident of the country for less than six months, they do not meet the residency requirement and are disqualified from voting.

6. C

Explanation: The Vice-President may be appointed to a Cabinet position without needing confirmation from the Commission on Appointments, as long as they are already elected and holding office.

7. B

Explanation: Civil rights are rights that protect individuals' freedom and ensure equal treatment under the law. These include rights such as privacy, family life, name, and security.

8. D

Explanation: According to Article XVII, Section 2 of the 1987 Constitution, amendments may be proposed through a people's initiative only after five years from the ratification of the Constitution.

9. A

Explanation: While the party-list system in the Philippines allows marginalized and underrepresented sectors like women, youth, labor, and peasants to have representation, religious groups are prohibited from registering as party-list organizations due to the principle of separation of Church and State.

10. A

Explanation: Under Article VII, Section 4 of the 1987 Constitution, the Supreme Court sitting en banc acts as the Sole Judge of all contests relating to the election, returns, and qualifications of the President and Vice-President.

11. C

Explanation: Only a judge can issue a warrant of arrest or search warrant, based on a personal determination of probable cause.

12. B

Explanation: While both are environmental issues, global warming is caused by greenhouse gases (e.g., CO₂), and ozone depletion is caused by CFCs. They are separate phenomena.

13. E

Explanation: Monoculture makes it easier for pests to spread, leading to increased infestations and reduced crop resilience.

14. A

Explanation: Dynamite fishing is highly destructive to coral reefs, killing fish and breaking coral structures.

15. E

Explanation: Unrestored or abandoned mined-out areas result in long-term land degradation and environmental damage.

16. B

Explanation: These behaviors reflect a lack of self-control and discipline in following rules and social norms.

17. B

Explanation: It's hard to control or limit access to marine resources, making them vulnerable to overuse.

18. D

Explanation: The Citizens Charter or service guide outlines services, requirements, fees, and processing time for transparency and efficiency.

19. C

Explanation: Filing of SALN (Statement of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth) is not mandatory for accepting a foreign scholarship.

20. D

Explanation: Standard government protocol often requires three signatures/initials for accountability: preparer, reviewer, and approver.

21. A

Explanation: Elective local officials in the Philippines serve three-year terms, with a maximum of three consecutive terms.

22. A

Explanation: The family is considered the foundation of the nation according to the Philippine Constitution.

23. C

Explanation: The CSC is one of the three Constitutional Commissions, along with COMELEC and COA.

24. A

Explanation: Only agricultural lands are alienable and disposable, meaning they can be owned or sold.

25. C

Explanation: The Generics Act of 1988 promotes affordable medicines and access to health services.

26. A

Explanation: Senators are not subject to impeachment. Only the President, Vice-President, Supreme Court Justices, Constitutional Commission members, and the Ombudsman are.

27. C

Explanation: Filipino is the national language of the Philippines, as stated in the Constitution.

28. A

Explanation: Taxes are compulsory payments imposed by the government to fund public services.

29. C

Explanation: The annual income tax return must be filed on or before April 15 of the following year.

30. B

Explanation: Individuals who earn money from employment or business activities pay income tax. It is levied on salaries, wages, professional fees, and other compensation.

31. D

Explanation: Only Congress, by a two-thirds vote, can declare a state of war.

32. B

Explanation: The Supreme Court may review the factual basis of martial law upon petition by a citizen.

33. C

Explanation: Due process of law, found in Article III, protects individuals from unfair treatment and deprivation of life, liberty, or property without legal procedures.

34. C

Explanation: Judicial review is a judicial power exercised by the Supreme Court.

35. B

Explanation: Treaties must have the concurrence of two-thirds of all Senators.

36. B

Explanation: Constitutional Commission members (COMELEC, COA, CSC) are appointed by the President without confirmation.

37. D

Explanation: All money bills must originate from the House of Representatives; the power of the purse belongs to Congress.

38. B

Explanation: The right against self-incrimination prevents compelled testimony that may lead to prosecution.

39. A

Explanation: Members of the House of Representatives serve a term of three years.

40. B

Explanation: An ex post facto law punishes an act that was not a crime when it was committed, and such laws are prohibited by the Constitution.
